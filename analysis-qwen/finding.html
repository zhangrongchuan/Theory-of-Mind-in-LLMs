Qwen ToM Findings: Figures 1-4

Simple figures for scaling, belief layers, deception, and method differences.

Finding 1

Methods with clear steps use larger models better

When the model gets bigger, VP and SoO improve only a little. SimToM and PercepToM improve much more.

Method0.6B1.7BDelta
VP 36.3% 39.7% +3.3
SoO 36.3% 40.7% +4.3
SimToM 40.7% 53.0% +12.3
PercepToM 40.7% 68.3% +27.7
Takeaway. Bigger models do not automatically solve ToM. They need clear reasoning steps to use their extra ability.
