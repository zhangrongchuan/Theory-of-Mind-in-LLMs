Why Order 4 Looks Easier Than Order 3

Qwen HiToM results show an order-4 bump in 7 of 8 runs. The bump is not evidence of stronger fourth-order Theory-of-Mind reasoning. It is driven by a label-space collapse toward initial/both target states and a matching prediction shortcut.

Category Definitions

How the four target-state classes are defined.

For each question, I first identify the target object and track only that object's physical locations in the story. The gold answer is then classified by its relation to the object's first location and final physical location.

Real example 1: melon, showing both and other. The melon starts in the blue_treasure_chest, then moves through green_bucket, green_drawer, and green_bottle, and finally Ava moves it back to the blue_treasure_chest.

So for the melon, initial = final = blue_treasure_chest. Questions whose gold answer is blue_treasure_chest are both. The question Where does Jacob really think the melon is? has gold green_drawer; that is other, because it is neither the first nor final physical location.

Real example 2: onion, showing initial-only and final-only. The onion starts in the red_crate. It is moved to red_drawer, red_bottle, back to red_crate, and later Isabella moves it to the blue_crate.

So for the onion, initial = red_crate and final = blue_crate. The question Where is the onion really? has gold blue_crate, so it is final-only. A nested-belief question such as Where does Isabella think Emily thinks Owen thinks the onion is? has gold red_crate, so it is initial-only.

initial-only

The gold answer is the first location, and the final location is different. Example: onion = red_crate.

both

The first location and final location are the same. Example: melon = blue_treasure_chest.

final-only

The gold answer is the final physical location, and it is not the first location. Example: onion = blue_crate.

other

The gold answer is neither the first nor the final physical location. Example: melon = green_drawer.

The real order-4 driver is the both target class.

This is more informative than the broad endpoint claim: order 4 is 100% endpoint, but not all endpoint cases are easy. The accuracy bump is concentrated in both, where the initial-state shortcut and the final-state shortcut collapse to the same answer.
Takeaway

Order 4 is a shortcut-aligned condition.

The result should not be interpreted as a monotonic improvement in fourth-order belief reasoning. The more defensible interpretation is that order-4 gold labels are entirely initial/both, and the models often shift toward those answers under order-4 wording. That shortcut is especially powerful for both targets, where initial and final are the same state.
